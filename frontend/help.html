Pickups

How do I request a pickup?

Log in, press New pickup request, choose the waste type, take a photo of it, enter an estimated weight and a time in the future, then press Submit request. An admin assigns a collector from your zone.

Why do I have to add a photo?

The photo shows the collector how much waste there is, so they bring the right bag or vehicle. Only you, the assigned collector and the admin can see it. Big photos are made smaller on your phone before they are sent.

Can I cancel a request?

Yes, while it is still Pending (before a collector is assigned). Open My requests, choose the request and press Cancel request. After that, open a support ticket.

The collector did not come. What should I do?

Check the request's timeline in My requests. If the time has passed, open a support ticket and choose that request; the admin will contact the collector or assign another one.

What can I recycle?

Plastic, paper and cardboard, e-waste, metal, glass and other recyclable waste. See today's rates on the home page. For "Other", describe what it is.

Points and cash

How are my points calculated?

Points = the actual weight the collector records × the category's rate on that day. They are added when the collector marks the pickup completed. Later rate changes do not change points you already earned.

How do I get money for my points?

Open Redeem, enter the points (at least the minimum shown there), and choose bKash, Nagad or a bank account. The points are held at once; an admin approves and sends the money, and you see the transaction ID when it is paid.

My redemption was rejected. Did I lose my points?

No. A rejected redemption gives all its points back to your balance, and the reason is shown under Redeem.

My points went down without a redemption. Why?

If the weight was recorded wrongly, an admin can correct it; the difference appears in Points as a "Weight correction". If you think it is wrong, open a support ticket.

Account

I forgot my password.

There is no self-service reset yet. Contact the admin by phone or email (below) from the number or email you registered with.

The login page says my account has been deactivated.

An admin has paused your account. Contact the admin (below) to find out why and to reactivate it.

I am a collector. How do I get an account?

Collector accounts are created by the admin, who also sets your zone. Contact the admin.
