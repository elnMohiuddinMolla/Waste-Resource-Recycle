Turn household recyclables into rewards

Request a doorstep pickup for plastic, paper, e-waste, metal or glass. A local collector weighs it on site, and you earn points you can cash out through bKash, Nagad or your bank.

How it works

  1. Request a pickup — choose the waste type, estimated weight and a time that suits you.
  2. A collector is assigned — someone from your zone accepts the job.
  3. Weighed at your door — the actual weight is recorded on a scale.
  4. Earn points — points = kg × the category's rate, credited when the pickup is completed.
  5. Cash out — redeem your points for money via bKash, Nagad or bank transfer.

Why WRR?

Valuable recyclables often end up in open dumps. WRR gives households a simple, trackable way to hand them over and gives collectors organised, zone-based work.
