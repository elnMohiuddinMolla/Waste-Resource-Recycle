How it works

From your doorstep to your wallet in four steps

  1. Request a pickup

    Pick the waste type, an estimated weight and a time that suits you. It takes under a minute.

  2. A local collector comes

    A collector from your zone accepts the job and calls before arriving. You follow every step online.

  3. Weighed at your door

    The actual weight goes on record. Points = kg × the category rate, credited the moment the pickup is completed.

  4. Cash out

    Redeem your points for money through bKash, Nagad or a bank transfer, and see the transaction ID when it is paid.
